Privacy Policy

Effective October 16, 2026

What the app collects

  • Videos you upload to the app. These are stored on the operator's home computer.
  • From TikTok, when you connect your account: your TikTok account ID (open_id) and display name; sign-in tokens that let the app send drafts to your inbox; and the upload status of each draft (processing, delivered to your inbox, or posted).

How it is used

  • To edit your videos, send finished edits to your TikTok inbox, and show which TikTok account is connected.
  • To check whether a draft reached your inbox or was posted.

The app does not read your other TikTok videos, followers, messages or analytics.

Where it is kept

  • Everything stays on the operator's computer. Video editing runs locally; no cloud AI services are used.
  • TikTok sign-in tokens are stored encrypted with Windows data protection and are never put in logs or web pages.

Sharing

  • Your edited video is sent only to TikTok, and only to your own inbox as a draft.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking or analytics.

How long it is kept

  • Videos are kept until the operator deletes them.
  • TikTok sign-in tokens are deleted when you disconnect TikTok.

Your choices

  • Disconnect TikTok at any time, in the app or in your TikTok settings.
  • To have your data deleted, email agutie149@gmail.com.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13.
