The app connects to TikTok through TikTok's official Login Kit and Content Posting API (upload to inbox). It never posts publicly. Every draft waits in your TikTok inbox until you choose to post it or delete it. When you use TikTok, TikTok's own Terms of Service and Community Guidelines apply.
