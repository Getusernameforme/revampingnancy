Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Kbot Digest Checker ("Kbot") is a personal tool used only by its operator with the operator's own Gmail account.

What Kbot accesses

  • Gmail, read-only. For messages in Sent mail on the current day, Kbot reads only the subject, the recipient address, the date and Gmail's labels. It does not read message bodies or attachments.
  • Kbot cannot send, change, label or delete email.

How it is used

  • To confirm that the operator's daily digest email (subject marked "[KBOT-RH Digest]") was sent to the operator's own address on each trading day.

Where it is kept

  • Everything stays on the operator's computer.
  • The Google sign-in token is stored encrypted with Windows data protection and is never written to logs.
  • Kbot keeps a local history of each check: the date, the result (for example "missing") and a short reason. It does not store email subjects, addresses or content.

Sharing

  • When a check fails, Kbot sends an alert to the operator's own ChatGPT account (OpenAI). The alert contains only the date, the result and a short reason, never email content.
  • Nothing is sold, shared with advertisers, or used for tracking or analytics.
  • Kbot's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

How long it is kept

  • Check history is kept until the operator deletes it.
  • The sign-in token is kept until access is revoked.

Your choices

  • Revoke Kbot's Gmail access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • To have data deleted, email agutie149@gmail.com.
