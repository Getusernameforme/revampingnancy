Kbot Digest Checker

Kbot is a personal tool that runs on its operator's home computer and works only with the operator's own Gmail account. It is not offered to the public.

On US stock-market trading days, Kbot checks whether the operator's daily trading digest email was sent. If it wasn't, or the check couldn't finish, Kbot posts a short alert to the operator's own ChatGPT account.

Kbot has read-only access to Gmail. It looks only at the subject, recipient and date of messages sent that day. It never reads message bodies or attachments, and it can't send, change or delete email.

See the privacy policy for details.
