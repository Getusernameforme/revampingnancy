Revamping Nancy

A desktop app that helps one creator turn her own phone videos into short TikTok videos. It edits her footage on her own computer, adds captions, and sends each finished video to her TikTok inbox as a draft.

She reviews every draft in TikTok and decides whether to post or delete it. The app never posts anything by itself.
